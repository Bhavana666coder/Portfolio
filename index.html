Repeat-Purchase Prediction

Project 5

Product Recommender: ALS vs. Popularity Baseline

  • PySpark
  • Spark MLlib
  • ALS
  • Collaborative filtering
  • Built a recommendation workflow on 8,755 filtered customer and product interactions using implicit-feedback ALS.
  • Compared ALS with a popularity baseline on Hit@10, Precision@10, NDCG@10 and catalog coverage.
  • The popularity baseline scored higher on the ranking metrics, while ALS covered about 10 times more of the catalog. Most customers buy once, so there is little history to learn from.
